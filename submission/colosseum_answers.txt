--- Project name ---
SettleNow

--- One-line pitch / tagline ---
SettleNow gives suppliers 90% of any invoice in USDC within seconds — upload, accept the quote, share a pay-link, vault closes on repayment.

--- Elevator pitch (short description) ---
Small suppliers wait 30-90 days to get paid, and factoring ignores small invoices. SettleNow advances ~90% in USDC instantly: upload the invoice, get a transparent deterministic quote (1.5% fee), the advance lands from an escrow vault, and the payer repays via a shareable pay-link that closes the vault. Live 60-second demo, no wallet or keys required.

--- Problem ---
B2B suppliers wait 30-90 days on invoices; traditional factoring is slow, paper-heavy, opaque on rates, and uneconomical under ~$10k. A $485 logistics invoice simply isn't factorable, so small suppliers absorb the cash-flow gap.

--- Solution ---
An invoice-advance vault: deterministic quote engine (tier base 92/90/82%, tenor/size haircuts, fee = base + 2bps/day capped 800bps) + Solana Anchor escrow vault (PDA per invoice, fund_advance / repay_close) + shareable payer pay-link + dashboard with devnet explorer links on both legs. UI and API share the same pure pricing function, so quotes can never disagree.

--- How it's built / tech stack ---
Next.js App Router + Solana wallet adapter + Anchor vault program + Supabase (post-hackathon persistence) + Vercel. Demo runs on deterministic mocks + localStorage (zero keys). Quote engine is a pure dependency-free TS function; demo ledger is SQLite (schema.sql + demo_seed.py, seed 20261006, $500 pilot cap, 1.5% fee asserts).

--- Tracks ---
Primary: Tempo. Secondary: Solana. Also: General/Open.

--- Why Tempo ---
Settlement-rail fit: instant cheap USDC advances and payer repayments; the pay-link checkout maps directly onto Tempo payments as the primary rail.

--- Why Solana ---
Anchor escrow vault (PDA per invoice, fund_advance / repay_close instructions), wallet-adapter advance claims, devnet explorer links on advance + repay legs; program at programs/settlenow/src/lib.rs (anchor build / cargo check pass).

--- Open-source & composability ---
Fully inspectable repo; quoteInvoice() is pure/dependency-free and exposed via POST /api/quote so any protocol can reuse pricing; per-invoice vault PDAs let lending/credit/insurance protocols compose on top. Deterministic seeds (seed vaults, tx sigs, pinnable `now`) make every run reproducible.
